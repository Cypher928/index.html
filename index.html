How is this score calculated?

What is the Trust Score? A 0–100 behavioral index derived from on-chain transaction patterns. It is recalculated each time you audit a wallet against the active rule set.

What raises the score: Regular payment activity, use of XRPL escrow, and transaction volume above minimum thresholds earn bonus points.

What lowers the score: High-risk transaction classifications, failed transactions, account deletion events, and detected AI agent automation patterns each carry configurable point deductions.

Score bands:
70–100 Trustworthy 40–69 Moderate Risk 0–39 High Risk

All thresholds are governed. Open the Rules panel (footer) to view and adjust every scoring parameter, severity level, and detection threshold.

⚠️ Disclaimer: This score is for informational and investigative purposes only. It is not a credit score, OFAC sanctions screen, legal compliance certification, or financial advice. Findings should be independently verified and reviewed by a qualified compliance professional before any regulatory filing or adverse action is taken. TrustLedger does not transmit wallet data to any server — all analysis runs locally in your browser.

About This Project
Why TrustLedger Exists

AI agents are increasingly authorized to move funds autonomously on XRPL — executing payments, managing escrows, and interacting with DeFi protocols without human approval for each transaction. Without explainability infrastructure, there is no way to audit what these agents did, verify their behavior was authorized, or satisfy compliance requirements after the fact.

TrustLedger provides compliance-grade observability for on-chain AI activity: governed detection rules, plain-English findings, structured investigation workflows, and exportable audit trails — built for a world where wallets may have no single human owner.
