About This Project
Why TrustLedger Exists

AI agents are increasingly authorized to move funds autonomously on XRPL — executing payments, managing escrows, and interacting with DeFi protocols without human approval for each transaction. Without explainability infrastructure, there is no way to audit what these agents did, verify their behavior was authorized, or satisfy compliance requirements after the fact.

TrustLedger provides compliance-grade observability for on-chain AI activity: governed detection rules, plain-English findings, structured investigation workflows, and exportable audit trails — built for a world where wallets may have no single human owner.
